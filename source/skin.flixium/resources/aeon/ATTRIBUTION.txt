Aeon MQ5 Home port for Flixium

Source: Aeon MQ 5 Omegamod, addon skin.aeonmq5.omegamod, version 13.8.2.
Original skin authors/providers: MarcosQui / scott967, with contributions from
the artists, font authors and translators credited in the original distribution.

The optional MQ5 Home uses adapted Home XML, assets and fonts from that source.
Flixium modifications include namespaced preferences/windows/includes/fonts,
isolated asset paths, Flixium-themed settings and selectors, a shared main-menu
editor and scoped provider adapters. Non-Home screens remain Flixium.

Original MQ5 skin content and adaptations are provided under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0. See LICENSE-MQ5.txt in this directory. Font-specific
licences and notices are retained with the fonts under fonts/mq5/.
MQ5 translations are distributed separately in Flixium Helper, with their own
attribution and the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence. They retain translator credits.

The source runtime version and window mapping are recorded in
runtime-manifest.json. The development source snapshot and conversion tools are
retained in the Flixium development tree, not in the installable addon ZIP.
